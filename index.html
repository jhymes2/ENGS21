On Belay?

An automated climbing wall sanitization system

ENGS 21 — Dartmouth Engineering

The Problem

Indoor climbing gym walls are difficult to clean, resulting in infrequent cleaning and the buildup of harmful bacteria — including what one study called a "fecal veneer" detectable on every wall surface tested (Bräuer et al., 2014). With over 695 commercial climbing gyms operating in the US in 2026 and 5.6 million indoor climbers, the exposure risk is significant.

Climbers spread bacteria via chalk-dusted hands and shoes, especially after restroom visits. Current cleaning methods — an employee on a spray bottle lowered down the wall, or an annual panel power-wash — are so labor-intensive that they are routinely skipped.

"Both existing solutions result in infrequent cleanings or a lack of thorough sanitation."

Our Solution

On Belay? is a rope-mounted cleaning device that clips into the existing top-rope belay infrastructure already present in every climbing gym. Two or more motor-powered oscillating brushes scrub chalk and residue from the wall surface as an employee slowly lowers the device. A spray system applies disinfectant, and a Shop-Vac attachment evacuates dislodged chalk dust — no new rail infrastructure required.

The device is controlled via an Arduino Nano managing relay outputs for the belay mechanism, brush motors, spray solenoid, and status light. Future revisions may add Bluetooth remote control (ZS-040 / HM-10 module) for hands-free operation.

Electronics & Hardware

The control system runs on an Arduino Nano (ATmega328) via PlatformIO. Four digital outputs manage the core actuators through relay modules:

ConstantPinFunction
PIN_RELAY_BELAYD2Belay motor relay
PIN_RELAY_BRUSHD3Oscillating brush motor relay
PIN_RELAY_SPRAYD4Disinfectant spray solenoid relay
PIN_SIGNAL_LIGHTD13 (LED_BUILTIN)Status indicator light

Built with the Arduino framework. Source available on GitHub.
